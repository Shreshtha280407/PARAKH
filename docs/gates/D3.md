## Actual statistics

Baseline conditions share the same simulation parameters with independent draws.
No assistance benefit is programmed. An identical-draw control is also supported
and tested. Differences below are assisted minus manual; exact agreement units
are percentages and difference units are percentage points.

| Metric | Manual | Rubric assisted | Difference | 95% package bootstrap CI for difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Mean pairwise QWK | 0.852201 | 0.849026 | −0.003175 | [−0.026922, 0.017650] |
| Fleiss kappa | 0.622386 | 0.610862 | −0.011524 | [−0.055643, 0.025082] |
| Exact agreement (%) | 62.680237 | 61.323155 | −1.357082 | [−5.850631, 2.267827] |
| Mean population rating SD | 0.191442 | 0.194547 | 0.003105 | [−0.015929, 0.027263] |
| MAE vs synthetic reference | 0.159457 | 0.156347 | −0.003110 | [−0.020954, 0.017218] |
| Computed-outcome Fleiss kappa | 0.249218 | 0.350766 | 0.101549 | [−0.153225, 0.380498] |
| Computed-outcome exact agreement (%) | 40 | 40 | 0 | [−20, 20] |

All seven differences have 2000 defined bootstrap replicates. Every interval
spans zero. Higher kappa/exact and lower SD/MAE are preferable, but these synthetic
numbers support no real-world performance conclusion. Outcome categories call
the existing production engine with its existing Decimal rounding and denominator
behavior; no outcome rule or threshold was rewritten.

Exclusions: 980 fixed-unscored package/PC items and 221 not-observable items in
either condition, leaving 1179 paired scored items. No missing rater slots or
reference labels occurred in the canonical fixture. Complete-case selection can
favor clearer evidence; the report gives raw status counts and the full ledger.
