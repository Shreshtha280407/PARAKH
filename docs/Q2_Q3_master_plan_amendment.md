# Owner-approved Q2/Q3 master-plan amendment — 2026-10-14

This amendment governs the Q2/Q3 delivery scope alongside the original
`KaushalPramaan_MVP_Master_Plan.pdf` (Q2/Q3/Q4 and IC-2/P8 sections). The supplied
PDF remains unchanged. The owner explicitly approved these decisions in this session.

1. Q2 provides deterministic Kotlin technical exposure, blur and temporal camera-motion
   assistance. Semantic task-target framing is unsupported and excluded from this scope;
   this does not claim successful automatic framing. Preserve and technically evaluate
   original Out_of_frame samples. A missing semantic target alone is not a technical defect.
2. Use only the original downloaded Drive proxy collection: 148 byte-unique photos and
   40 byte-unique clips. No additional capture sets, field-data request, model training or
   fine-tuning. This replaces the earlier agreement to collect two independent sets.
3. Deliver functional offline assistance, validated intake/review provenance, versioned
   deterministic threshold calibration/configuration, Kotlin/reference parity, meaningful
   unit/instrumented tests, CI-safe synthetic fixtures and actual USB verification on the
   available OnePlus 13R. Calibrate/evaluate on the available proxy collection, explicitly
   as a calibration/resubstitution technical evaluation. Repeated scenes remain grouped;
   do not claim independent calibration/validation/holdout partitions or field generalisation.
4. Record actual proxy confusion matrices, denominators, unjudgeable coverage and errors.
   Original held-out Q2 precision/recall and Q3 agreement requirements are not proved by
   reuse of calibration footage. Numeric proxy results are descriptive, not an independent
   performance gate. Scope completion requires real functioning modules and supporting
   tests/evidence, not merely a successful build or files being present.
5. Available hardware verification is the connected OnePlus 13R, a flagship device. Report
   its actual timings/memory and tested source; do not call it entry-level or mid-range.
   Original representative entry/mid-device guarantees, Q3 <=60ms on mid-range and IC-2
   <=5s/video minute on entry hardware remain unverified and are not accepted by this
   amendment. Their absence is a disclosed boundary of the completed functional scope.
6. Q4/P8 will integrate completed assistance outputs, capture guidance and human review
   with later manifests/storage/timelines. It does not promise automatic semantic framing.
   Any future automatic task-target framing needs separate defined design and validation.

Proxy household/switch/MCB footage is not electrician competence or real-trade validation.
Hand presence is not proof of work, interaction, safety, identity or competence. Do not
change production assessment/capture/seal/sync/scoring flows in Q2/Q3. Preserve originals,
root `.env`, project/phone data and assessments. No commit/push/PR is authorised.

The [combined acceptance record](gates/Q2_Q3_progress.md) and Q2/Q3 gates must distinguish
completion of this amended scope from unproved original-plan claims. Hosted CI remains
